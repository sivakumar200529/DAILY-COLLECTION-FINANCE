## 🌟 1. Core Financial Model (100-Day Dynamic Formula)

The system strictly enforces precision-safe microfinance mathematics isolating **Requested Amount** from **Disbursed Principal**:

```text
Requested Amount    = ₹10,000
Disbursed Principal = ₹8,800
Daily Collection    = ₹100
Collection Period   = 100 days

Total Target Repayment
= Daily Collection × Collection Period
= ₹100 × 100
= ₹10,000

Finance Margin Yield
= Total Repayment − Disbursed Principal
= ₹10,000 − ₹8,800
= ₹1,200
```

## 👥 2. Three Distinct Roles & Personas

1. **Administrator (Full System Controls)**:
   - Complete master access: edit/delete accounts, manage collection plans, areas, collectors, audits, customer profiles, and system settings.
   - Comprehensive Analytics Dashboard with 10 financial KPI metrics and 4 Recharts visual distribution charts.

2. **Collection Agent (Field Operations)**:
   - Doorstep collection workspace with route sequence ordering.
   - 1-Tap Quick Collect with instant remaining balance recalculation.
   - Cash collection, missed days recording, and customer repayment history.
   - Bluetooth 58mm/80mm ESC/POS thermal receipt printing & instant WhatsApp sharing.

3. **Customer Self-Service Portal**:
   - Live repayment passbook tracking paid, remaining, and percentage completed.
   - **Razorpay Online Repayment**: Instant self-repayment via Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, UPI QR, and NetBanking (HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis).
   - In-person cash collection advisory.
   - Digital KYC document uploads and verification tracking.
